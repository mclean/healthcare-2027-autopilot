Reading the pattern

  • Self-pay share of visits: Former Medicaid and exchange patients arriving uninsured.
  • Coverage-related denial rate: Eligibility checked too early, too rarely or not at all.
  • Point-of-service collection rate: Balances that leave the building unpaid become bad debt at a much higher rate.
  • Bad debt plus charity, % of gross revenue: The lagging confirmation of churn and collection gaps.
  • Contracts inside their notice window without a prep file: The leverage moment is passing.
  • Cash-critical vendors without a tested backup: One outage away from a cash stoppage.
